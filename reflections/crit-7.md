# Crit 7 reflection

## 1. What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

I don't like how the timetabling app works when we select labs or tutorials. I
have to choose from a table of options and I can't see what is free and full in
my timetable, and it depends on my memory and a word "clash" but doesn't show
how much clash, how much is left etc. So my app shows the options as an overlay
and shows the live count for students to know how fast the class fills and what
is full.

## 2. What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

Shows the classes while choosing labs and makes the user's understanding of
their timetable easy and better to choose.
